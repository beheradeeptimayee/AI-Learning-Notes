AI: Artificial Intelligence
---------------------------------
-> A Technology which is used to perform task which requires human intelligence.

-> AI can understand data, identify patterns, recognize speech or images,
and make decisions or predictions.

Examples of AI:
------------------
 (1)Pattern recognition:-
AI can identify different kinds of patterns from a large amounts of data.

Ex: (i) ChatGPT identifies patterns in text and generates relevant responses.
(ii) Recommendation systems identify patterns in what users watch or buy.


(2) Speech Recogntition
AI can understand human speech and convert it into text or commands.

Ex:ChatGPT,Gemini,Siri etc.(are AI assistants that use multiple AI technologies, including speech recognition when you interact with them by voice.)
-> "Set an alarm for 1PM for Meeting"
The AI recognizes our speech and understands the command.

(3)Image Recognition
AI can analyze an image or video and identify objects, people,
vehicles, signs, or other information.

Ex: Traffic Scene
A traffic camera captures a vehicle crossing a red light.
AI can recognize:
- Vehicle
- Traffic signal
- Number plate
- Red-light violation

Based on the detected violation, the system can automatically
generate a traffic challan.

Conclusion:

AI → Takes Data → Finds Patterns → Understands/Recognizes → Gives Output

** Hierarchy **
------------------

           Artificial Intelligence (AI)
        (Broad field of creating intelligent systems that can perform tasks like humans.)
                            ↓
                Machine Learning (ML)
        (A subdomain of Al where systems learn patterns from data without being explicitly programmed)
                            ↓
                Deep Learning (DL)
        (A subdomain of ML that uses neural networks with many layers to learn from large amounts of data)
                            ↓
                Generative AI (GenAI)

       (A subdomain of DL that focuses on generating new content (text, images, audio, code, etc.) based on learned patterns.)
